Game Mechanics

No Scripted Dialogues. No Fixed Solutions.

Every escape room quiz in Tywo is generated with bespoke constraints. You don't click multiple-choice options — you interrogate, convince, bargain, and outsmart living LLM-backed actors.

psychology_alt

1. Emergent NPC Minds

NPCs possess hidden memories, personal biases, and emotional triggers. Catch them in contradictions or appeal to their sympathies to reveal door ciphers.

NPC Warder: "I swore never to speak of Sector 4... but your logic regarding the power grid is hard to ignore."
key

2. Dynamic Escape Quizzes

Riddles and room mechanics adapt to your player style. If you are aggressive, security tightens. If you solve puzzles cleverly, the environment reacts dynamically.

Room Puzzle: Procedurally linked to ancient mathematical paradoxes and current room state.
lock_clock

3. High Stakes Timer Loop

Every question, dialogue turn, and failed attempt costs room oxygen and battery reserves. Rapid, clever deductions are critical to survive.

Environmental metric: Oxygen 38% | Security Alert: LEVEL 2 | Escape probability: 64%
Behind The Scenes

Hybrid Deterministic / Generative Engine

How do you stop LLMs from breaking escape room rules or hallucinating keys that don't exist? Tywo combines rigid deterministic state machines (locks, inventory, physics) with generative narrative models.

State Guardrails & Verification

NPC dialogue cannot hallucinate door unlocks unless the verification engine confirms mathematical conditions are met.

Dynamic Psychology State Machine

NPCs track suspicion, fear, and admiration metrics that directly color their token generation prompts in real time.

Zero Pre-Written Dialogues

Every interaction is authentically negotiated. Your creativity defines your escape path.
