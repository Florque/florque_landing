
## Florque AI Studio Overview

**Florque AI Studio** serves as an experimental AI research and development hub focused on researching novel ideas, engineering functional POCs, and sharing public progress. Alongside core R&D, the studio acts as a living portfolio demonstrating capabilities in AI agent architecture, workflow automation, and interactive systems.

---

## Core R&D & Portfolio Projects

| Project | Target Scope | Core Innovation & Value Proposition | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Florque** | Program Management Agent | Full-scale, work graph–based, AI-powered ticket tracking and self-healing execution engine. Automates, monitors, and optimizes project operations for teams. | **WIP** |
| **Florque Lite** | Idea-to-Execution Planner | Mobile-first AI planning agent. Transforms raw ideas into actionable, resource-constrained execution plans and dynamically adapts the roadmap based on new inputs. | **WIP** |
| **Tywo** | Interactive Gaming / AI NPCs | AI-powered escape room quiz game where non-player characters (NPCs) leverage LLM backends for emergent, unpredictable, and dynamic gameplay logic. | **WIP** |

---

## Studio Structure & Objectives

* **Primary Objective (R&D & POC Sharing):** Rapidly prototype, validate, and document complex AI architectures (graph-based execution, dynamic agentic planning, and LLM-driven game loops).
* **Supplementary Objective (Portfolio & Contracting):** Demonstrate hands-on engineering capability to clients and employers seeking expertise in multi-agent orchestration, adaptive workflows, and custom LLM integrations.
